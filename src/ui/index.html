      Set up the storage (once)

      Footage is big, so it goes in your own free Backblaze storage: 10 GB free, no card needed. Projects get a folder of their own there, apart from Film assets.

      1. Make a free account on (pick B2 Cloud Storage), or use the one you made for Film assets.
      2. On the left click Application Keys, then Generate New Master Application Key.
      3. Copy the keyID and the applicationKey into the boxes here and click Set up.

      The app makes a private folder there and two keys only for it. Your master key isn't saved anywhere.

      The owner is setting up the storage for Projects. You can still join projects friends invite you to.

      Sign in with Google (set up once)

      YouTube only shares watch time and where viewers come from with apps you make yourself in Google Cloud. It's free and you only do it once.

      1. Make a project. Open , sign in with the account your channel is on, name it VaultHub and click Create.
      2. Turn on YouTube. Click and press Enable. Do the same for .
      3. Name the app. Open the , click Get started, type VaultHub and your email, pick External, and finish. Then go to Audience and click Publish app (so Google doesn't sign you out every week).
      4. Make the key. Go to , pick Desktop app, click Create, and copy the Client ID and Client secret into the boxes below.

      When you sign in, Google says the app isn't checked by Google. That's normal for an app you made yourself: click Advanced, then Go to VaultHub. The app can only look at your numbers, never change anything.
